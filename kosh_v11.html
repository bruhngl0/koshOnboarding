Your box

Not a container.
A commitment.

Your Kōsh box is ceramic-coated glass — built for food, not plastic. It is made specifically for you, with your name on it. At the end of your subscription, it does not go back to us. It goes home with you. Permanently yours.

01

Ceramic-coated glass, made for food

No leaching, no plastic, no compromise. Designed to hold heat, keep food fresh, and look like something worth opening.

02

Your name. Only yours.

Every box is individual to the member it is made for. No two are the same. A new member gets a new box — your box is never reused.

03

Portioned to your numbers

Same kitchen. Same menu. Different portion sizes, calibrated to your calorie target and macro split each day.

04

Yours forever at the end

When your subscription ends, the box is handed over to you. A lasting record of the cycle you committed to.
